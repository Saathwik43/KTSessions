BLUE LANTERN COWORKING - MEMBER HANDBOOK  

Welcome
Blue Lantern Coworking is a shared workspace in Hyderabad, located on the 4th floor of Mayuri Towers, Madhapur. It was founded in 2021 by Kavya Reddy and Arjun Mehta. The space has 120 desks, 6 meeting rooms and a 40-seat event hall called The Lighthouse.

Opening Hours
The space is open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays it is open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Members on the Night Owl plan get 24x7 access using their access card. The space is closed on Diwali, Sankranti and Independence Day.

Membership Plans
There are three plans. The Hot Desk plan costs Rs. 4,500 per month and gives you any free desk in the open area. The Fixed Desk plan costs Rs. 7,200 per month and gives you your own desk with a lockable drawer. The Night Owl plan costs Rs. 9,000 per month and includes a fixed desk plus 24x7 access. All plans require a refundable security deposit of Rs. 3,000. Students get a 20 percent discount on the Hot Desk plan when they show a valid college ID.

Meeting Rooms
Meeting rooms are booked through the Lantern app. Every member gets 5 free meeting room hours per month. After that, a room costs Rs. 300 per hour. The largest room, called Deep Sea, seats 12 people and has a video conferencing setup. Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the start time.

Internet and Printing
The WiFi network is called BlueLantern-Members and the speed is 300 Mbps. The WiFi password changes on the first day of every month and is sent to members by email. Each member can print 50 black and white pages per month for free. Extra pages cost Rs. 2 per page, and colour prints cost Rs. 10 per page.

Guests
Members can bring up to 2 guests per day. Guests must sign in at the front desk and can stay for a maximum of 3 hours. Guests are not allowed in the space after 8:00 PM.

Food and Pantry
Tea, coffee and filter water are free for all members. The pantry is restocked every morning at 8:00 AM. A catered lunch is available on weekdays for Rs. 120 per meal and must be ordered in the Lantern app before 10:30 AM. Please do not eat at the desks in the Quiet Zone.

Pets
Dogs are welcome on Fridays only, which we call Furry Friday. Pets must stay on a leash and are not allowed in meeting rooms or the pantry.

Cancellation
To cancel a membership, give 15 days of written notice by emailing help@bluelantern.example. The security deposit is refunded within 10 working days after the access card is returned. A lost access card costs Rs. 500 to replace.

Support
The community manager is Farah Khan. She is at the front desk from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays. For urgent issues outside those hours, call the building security desk on extension 104.
